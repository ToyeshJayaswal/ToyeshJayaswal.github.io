YouTube!

In high school, we were tasked to work on a personal project for 100 hours. As I learned a lot from YouTube, I wanted to make my own channel to share what I learned with others. I try to make new videos when I have free time, but that end up being not very often. PROMYS

PROMYS is a 6 week summer program at Boston University where high schools students learn topics in elementary number theory, advanced topics from visiting professors, and work on exploration or research projects. I was a student in 2020 and have been a counselor twice in 2022 and 2024. The six weeks are spent doing and talking about math basically from the moment you wake up to when you go to sleep. It's been an invaluable experience of mine, and I've become friends with some of the most talented people I know because of it.
