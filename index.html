About Me

I am currently a second year mathematics graduate student at Stony Brook University, and before that, I completed my undergraduate degree from Caltech. I am very interested in learning primarily about geometry, but also physics and number theory, and how these fields play off of one another. My particular interests include mathematical gauge theory, algebraic topology, L-functions and modular forms, and an attempt to understand QFT. What excites me most are results that combine various perspectives and a priori unrelated ideas. I hope that my research will also echo this sentiment.

On this website, you can select the tabs to find some notes of mine, my YouTube channel where I have tried to share some things I have learned, and an extensive reading list of math and physics textbooks that I have at least partially read and thoroughly enjoyed.
